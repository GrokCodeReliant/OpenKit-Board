# Kit Sparks

**A tiny skirmish rules sample for Open Kit Board.**  
Author: Open Kit / GrokCodeReliant · **License: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)**  
Use freely. This is an original micro-system for demos and AI helper tests — not a clone of any commercial RPG.

---

## What you need

- One character sheet per hero (below).
- A handful of six-sided dice (**d6**).
- Tokens on the board for heroes and foes.

---

## Character sheet

Write these on the piece sheet / notes card:

| Field | What it means |
|--------|----------------|
| **Name** | Who they are |
| **Role** | **Fighter** or **Rogue** |
| **Fight** | Hitting and holding ground (usually 1–3) |
| **Sneak** | Stealth, traps, finesse (usually 1–3) |
| **Grit** | Staying power; also starting **HP** = 3 + Grit |
| **Armor** | 0–2. Subtract from damage taken (min 0 damage left) |

Keep totals modest: **Fight + Sneak + Grit ≤ 5** at start.

---

## How to try something

1. Say what you attempt.
2. Pick **Fight** or **Sneak** (or **Grit** if enduring something).
3. Roll **1d6 + that stat**.
4. **5+** succeeds. **9+** is a strong success (extra effect if it makes sense). **4 or less** fails or succeeds with a cost (DM picks).

Opposed: both sides roll; higher total wins (tie → attacker glances / no lasting change).

---

## Damage & HP

- A solid hit deals **1** damage, or **2** on a strong success / brutal weapon.
- **Armor** reduces that damage (never below 0).
- At **0 HP**, the hero is **down** (out of the fight until an ally spends an action to stabilize — then 1 HP and limited).

---

## Roles & actions

Each turn in a scrap, a hero picks **one** action (plus short free talk).

### Fighter

- **Strike** — Fight check to deal damage to a foe in reach.
- **Guard** — Until your next turn, you or an adjacent ally take −1 damage from the next hit (after Armor).
- **Shove** — Fight check to push a foe one cell / break their position.

### Rogue

- **Skulk** — Sneak check to become **Hidden** (next Strike from Hidden is +1 and deals +1 damage if it hits; then Hidden ends).
- **Strike** — Fight check; from Hidden, use the bonus above.
- **Disable** — Sneak check to drop a simple trap, lock, or gadget on the board (DM sets the target).

---

## Foes (quick)

Give a foe **Fight**, **HP** (2–5), **Armor** (0–1). They Strike or move; no need for full sheets.

---

## That’s it

Board + tokens + this folio. Import your own deeper rules anytime — Kit Sparks is only the built-in spark for testing.
